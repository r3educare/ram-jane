This is my reading of the direction, not a prediction or source-backed forecast. Check the sources in the next tab.
  • AI as a teammate: one person with AI tools can now do the work of a small team (content, support, analysis, coding). The advantage moves to people with domain knowledge and trust, which you have in maths teaching.
  • Personalised learning: demand for practice, feedback and doubt-solving tailored to each student, at lower cost than coaching.
  • Vernacular content: Hindi and regional-language education and wellness content is under-served relative to the audience.
  • Creator + community economy: small paying communities, cohorts and newsletters instead of huge audiences.
  • Wellness and mental health: growing awareness, so meditation, stress and focus programmes are in demand.
  • Data and AI skills: steady demand for analysts and AI-literate professionals; also for teachers who can explain them simply.
  • Risk: low barriers mean many competitors; trust, niche and consistency decide who survives. Platform changes (YouTube, AI tools) can hurt overnight.
