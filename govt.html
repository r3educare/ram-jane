General information only, not legal advice. In brief: you can ask police to register an FIR (zero-FIR at any station, free copy of FIR); an arrested person must be told the grounds, may consult a lawyer, and be produced before a magistrate within 24 hours; you can ask for records under RTI. Confirm current rules (India’s new criminal laws, BNSS 2023, replaced CrPC from July 2024) with a lawyer or the official text.
